Professional Summary

I began my professional journey at the age of 16 working in the IT department of a company, where I quickly developed technical and problem-solving skills beyond my initial responsibilities. This early experience motivated me to pursue a career in the game industry, leading me to earn a degree in Game Design.

During my studies, I secured an internship in game development, gaining practical experience collaborating with development teams and working on real projects. After graduating, I joined a company focused on Augmented Reality, contributing to interactive experiences and concept demonstrations for clients.

Today, I work as a QA Tester in the game industry, where I perform functional, exploratory, and regression testing across multiple builds and platforms. I work with professional QA workflows using tools such as Jira, TestRail, and Confluence, focusing on bug investigation, reproduction steps, and improving overall game stability and player experience.

Alongside my QA work, I continue developing game projects, participating in game jams, and experimenting with engines such as Unity, Unreal Engine, GameMaker, and Construct, combining design, development, and testing knowledge to better understand the full game production pipeline.
