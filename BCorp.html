B Corp

Type: Final Project – 3D Adventure Game

B Corp is a 3D adventure game set in a post-apocalyptic world where the disappearance of bees causes a global environmental crisis.

My Role

B Corp was my graduation project, developed as part of a team. I had a strong role in the game's design and narrative direction.

Game Design Level Design Narrative Design Gameplay Mechanics Character Design Dialogue Design Minigames

My Contribution

I was responsible for several aspects of the game's design, including its story, gameplay mechanics, characters, dialogues, minigames and level design.

I also contributed to the game's design documentation and helped shape how the different elements of the experience connected with the game's narrative and progression.

Design Challenge

One of the main challenges was creating a post-apocalyptic world that felt different from more conventional settings while making the player understand the importance of bees to the environment.

Instead of using the disappearance of bees only as a narrative element, the game's universe was designed around the consequences of their absence.

Design Decisions

A major design decision was to build the game's universe around the question:

What would the world look like if bees disappeared?

This helped guide the game's narrative, worldbuilding and gameplay direction, while also giving the project an identity beyond a conventional post-apocalyptic setting.

Level Design

I worked on the level design, focusing on the structure of the player's progression, level layout and how challenges were presented throughout the experience.

My goal was to make the player gradually understand the world and its central premise while progressing through the game.

Gameplay & Minigames

I designed gameplay mechanics and minigames that contributed to the player's interaction with the world and supported the game's themes.

These systems were designed to make the player interact with the game's world rather than relying only on exposition to communicate its message.

What I Would Improve

If I were to continue developing B Corp, I would expand the game with additional missions and progression before introducing the main mission.

This would give players more time to explore the world, understand its context and become more familiar with the game's mechanics before progressing into the main storyline.
